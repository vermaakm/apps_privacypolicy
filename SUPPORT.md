# Purrsuit Support

Purrsuit is published by MCV Limited.

## Contact

Email **mcvlimited.uk@gmail.com** with “Purrsuit support” in the subject line.

Please include a short description, the device model, iOS version, Purrsuit version, and a screenshot if it contains no private tracker information. Do not send passwords, full payment-card information, Apple authorization codes, or sensitive tracker content.

## Subscriptions

Purrsuit Pro subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. To view, change, or cancel one, open **iPhone or iPad Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Purrsuit**, or visit https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

Deleting a Purrsuit account does not automatically cancel an active App Store subscription.

## Restore a purchase

Sign in with the same Purrsuit account and Apple ID used for the purchase, open the **Account** tab, and use the restore option on the subscription screen or customer centre.

## Delete an account

In Purrsuit, open **Account > Delete account**. The app asks you to authenticate again and confirm permanent deletion. This removes the Purrsuit account and its associated tracker data and cannot be undone.

For help with deletion or a privacy request, email **mcvlimited.uk@gmail.com** with “Purrsuit privacy request” in the subject line.
