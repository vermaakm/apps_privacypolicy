# MCV Limited Apps Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 4, 2026
**Contact:** mcvlimited.uk@gmail.com

This is the umbrella Privacy Policy for mobile applications, games, websites, and related services published or operated by MCV Limited that link to it (collectively, the "Apps"). It explains how MCV Limited and the service providers used across our portfolio handle information when you use an App.

Not every App uses every feature or service described below. For example, one App may use AdMob but not RevenueCat, while another may use Firebase without advertising. An App's store listing, in-app notices, permissions, and any app-specific section below identify the relevant features. If an app-specific notice conflicts with this umbrella policy for a particular App, the app-specific notice applies for that App.

## Information we may collect

Depending on the App and the features you use, we or our service providers may process:

- **Account and profile information**, such as an email address, name, username, authentication-provider identifier, or profile image, if an App offers accounts or sign-in.
- **Content and information you provide**, such as entries, preferences, messages, records, photos, files, or other information you choose to save or upload in an App.
- **Purchase and subscription information**, such as entitlement status, product identifiers, receipts, purchase tokens, and transaction information. We do not receive or store full payment-card details for app-store purchases.
- **Device, usage, and diagnostic information**, such as device type, operating-system version, app version, IP address, app interactions, advertising or device identifiers, and diagnostic information, where collected by an App or its service providers.
- **Location information** only when an App expressly requests access to it or explains its use in an app-specific notice.

## How we use information

We may use information to provide and improve Apps; authenticate users; save and synchronize content; manage purchases and subscriptions; provide support; protect against fraud, abuse, and security incidents; comply with law; and, where an App contains advertising, display, measure, limit, and where allowed personalize ads.

## Advertising: Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads

Some Apps use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banner, interstitial, rewarded, native, or other advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process IP address, device and advertising identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and, where permitted, ad personalization.

Where required, an App uses Google's consent flow before requesting ads and provides a privacy-options entry point when Google requires one. You can use that entry point to review or change applicable advertising choices. Declining consent may result in non-personalized, limited, or no ads, depending on availability and applicable law.

On Apple devices, an App may also display Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you allow tracking, Google and its advertising partners may access the device advertising identifier (IDFA) to provide and measure personalized advertising and help detect fraud. If you choose **Ask App Not to Track**, the App remains usable and ads may still be shown without the IDFA. You can change this permission later in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking.

See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Advertising practices can vary between Apps. An App may show contextual, non-personalized, limited, or personalized advertising depending on the user's consent, age-related settings, platform permissions, region, and ad availability. Apps intended for children will use the applicable child-directed treatment and advertising restrictions.

## Subscriptions: RevenueCat

Some Apps use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and purchase entitlements. RevenueCat may process device and technical information, purchase and transaction information such as receipts or purchase tokens, usage information, and any App User IDs or customer attributes that the relevant App is configured to send. RevenueCat does not process full payment-card details for app-store purchases.

RevenueCat information is used to validate purchases, prevent fraud, restore purchases, determine access to paid features, provide customer support, and produce subscription analytics. Depending on the App, the RevenueCat App User ID may be anonymous or may be linked to an account identifier. Purchases are completed by Apple, Google, or another applicable app store, which processes payment information under its own privacy policy.

See [RevenueCat's Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

## Cloud features: Supabase

Some Apps use Supabase to provide services such as authentication, database storage, file storage, realtime features, or server functions. In those Apps, Supabase processes the account information, app content, and technical data sent to it so that it can provide the requested feature. We configure access controls intended to limit users to the information they are authorized to access.

See [Supabase's Privacy Policy](https://supabase.com/privacy).

## Google Firebase

Some Apps use one or more Google Firebase services. These may include Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, Cloud Messaging, Remote Config, App Check, or related Firebase infrastructure.

Depending on the App and features used, Firebase may process:

- Email address, display name, user ID, and third-party authentication-provider identifiers for account access.
- Content, preferences, files, records, or other information a user asks an App to store or synchronize.
- Device, operating-system, app-version, language, IP address, installation, and technical information.
- App interactions and usage events where Analytics is enabled.
- Crash logs, stack traces, performance measurements, and diagnostic information where Crashlytics or Performance Monitoring is enabled.
- Push-notification tokens and notification interactions where Cloud Messaging is enabled.
- Security and attestation information where App Check or abuse-prevention tools are enabled.

Firebase information may be used for authentication, cloud storage and synchronization, app functionality, analytics, diagnostics, performance, notifications, configuration, fraud prevention, abuse prevention, and security. We configure access controls intended to restrict private account content to authorized users. The exact Firebase products and data practices applicable to a particular App depend on that App's features and store disclosures.

See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Firebase privacy and security information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## App-specific information: Purrsuit

Purrsuit is a personal tracking App published by MCV Limited. It is not intended to provide medical, financial, or other professional advice.

Purrsuit processes the following information according to the features you use:

- **Account information:** email address and Firebase user identifier, plus an authentication-provider identifier when you use Sign in with Apple or Google. Apple or Google may provide a name on the first authorization. Purrsuit does not receive your Apple ID or Google password.
- **Tracker content:** tracker names, categories, units, optional notes and targets, dated numeric entries, and settings. Live trackers may also contain a selected place, sports team, market symbol, or related source parameters. This information is used to provide the tracking features you request.
- **Language and settings:** preferred language, advertising-consent choices, and other operational preferences.
- **Subscriptions:** RevenueCat receives a Purrsuit App User ID linked to the Firebase user identifier, product and entitlement identifiers, App Store receipt and transaction information, and technical information needed to determine whether Purrsuit Pro is active. MCV Limited and RevenueCat do not receive full payment-card information.
- **Advertising:** free users may see Google AdMob banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Google and its partners may process IP address, advertising identifiers including IDFA when Apple tracking permission and applicable consent have been granted, app interactions, consent signals, and diagnostic or fraud-prevention information.
- **Live-data requests:** when a user chooses a live tracker, Purrsuit sends the necessary query, such as a location coordinate, team identifier, or market symbol, to the relevant public data source. Purrsuit does not request precise device-location permission; the user chooses the location or subject to track.

Purrsuit uses Firebase Authentication for account access, Cloud Firestore for eligible cloud storage and synchronization, RevenueCat for subscription status and purchase restoration, Apple App Store for payment, and Google AdMob/User Messaging Platform for advertising and privacy choices.

Free-tier tracking content may be stored only on the user's device. Purrsuit Pro content may be stored in Cloud Firestore so it can synchronize across signed-in devices. Removing the App can erase device-only content. Cloud storage eligibility and migration behavior are described in the App when applicable.

Purrsuit does not publicly display tracker content and does not provide social sharing or messaging. MCV Limited does not sell Purrsuit tracker content.

Users can permanently delete their Purrsuit account from **Account > Delete account**. The deletion flow re-authenticates the user, removes tracker content and settings held in Purrsuit's Firebase database, revokes Apple authorization where applicable, and deletes the Firebase Authentication account. Apple subscription and transaction records are controlled separately by Apple and may be retained where required for financial, fraud-prevention, tax, or legal obligations. Deleting a Purrsuit account does not automatically cancel an active App Store subscription; subscriptions can be managed in Apple ID settings.

For access, correction, portability, deletion, or privacy questions, contact **mcvlimited.uk@gmail.com**. Include “Purrsuit privacy request” in the subject line. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or sensitive tracker content by email.

## Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the relevant App, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and maintain security. Retention varies by App and by the data involved. Where an App supports accounts, it may provide an in-app deletion option. You can also contact us at the email below to request deletion or ask how deletion works for any MCV Limited App.

Device-only information remains on the device until it is deleted in the App, the account is deleted, or the App and its local data are removed. Cloud account content is retained while the account is active and is removed through the App's account-deletion flow, subject to backups, security logs, transaction records, and information that must be retained for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes. Service providers may retain limited records under their own legal obligations and retention schedules.
