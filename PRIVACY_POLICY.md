# MCV Limited Apps Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 3, 2026  
**Contact:** mcvlimited.uk@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy applies to mobile applications, games, and related services published by MCV Limited that link to this policy (collectively, the "Apps"). It explains how information is handled when you use an App.

Not every App uses every feature or service described below. An App's store listing, in-app notices, permissions, and any app-specific privacy notice explain features that apply to that App. If an app-specific notice conflicts with this policy for a particular App, the app-specific notice applies.

## Information we may collect

Depending on the App and the features you use, we or our service providers may process:

- **Account and profile information**, such as an email address, name, username, authentication-provider identifier, or profile image, if an App offers accounts or sign-in.
- **Content and information you provide**, such as entries, preferences, messages, records, photos, files, or other information you choose to save or upload in an App.
- **Purchase and subscription information**, such as entitlement status, product identifiers, receipts, purchase tokens, and transaction information. We do not receive or store full payment-card details for app-store purchases.
- **Device, usage, and diagnostic information**, such as device type, operating-system version, app version, IP address, app interactions, advertising or device identifiers, and diagnostic information, where collected by an App or its service providers.
- **Location information** only when an App expressly requests access to it or explains its use in an app-specific notice.

## How we use information

We may use information to provide and improve Apps; authenticate users; save and synchronize content; manage purchases and subscriptions; provide support; protect against fraud, abuse, and security incidents; comply with law; and, where an App contains advertising, display, measure, limit, and where allowed personalize ads.

## Advertising: Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads

Some Apps use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banner, interstitial, rewarded, native, or other advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process IP address, device and advertising identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and, where permitted, ad personalization.

Where required, an App uses Google's consent flow before requesting ads and provides a privacy-options entry point when Google requires one. You can use that entry point to review or change applicable advertising choices. Declining consent may result in non-personalized, limited, or no ads, depending on availability and applicable law.

See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Subscriptions: RevenueCat

Some Apps use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and purchase entitlements. RevenueCat may process device and technical information, purchase and transaction information such as receipts or purchase tokens, usage information, and any App User IDs or customer attributes that the relevant App is configured to send. RevenueCat does not process full payment-card details for app-store purchases.

See [RevenueCat's Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

## Cloud features: Supabase

Some Apps use Supabase to provide services such as authentication, database storage, file storage, realtime features, or server functions. In those Apps, Supabase processes the account information, app content, and technical data sent to it so that it can provide the requested feature. We configure access controls intended to limit users to the information they are authorized to access.

See [Supabase's Privacy Policy](https://supabase.com/privacy).

## Other service providers and sharing

An App may use additional providers for app-store payments, authentication, crash reporting, analytics, notifications, email, or customer support. We may share information with those providers only as needed to operate the relevant feature, comply with law, protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transfer. We do not sell personal information.

## Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the relevant App, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and maintain security. Retention varies by App and by the data involved. You may be able to delete information within an App; you can also contact us at the email below to request deletion or ask how to delete information for a particular App.

## Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or portability of personal information, and to object to certain processing. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To make a request, contact us at **mcvlimited.uk@gmail.com**. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

## International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

## Children

Unless an App states otherwise, our Apps are not directed to children under 13 or the minimum age required in the user's jurisdiction, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children below that age. An App directed to children will provide an app-specific notice and use the applicable age, advertising, and parental-consent controls.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices, features, or legal requirements change. We will post the updated version and revise the effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email **mcvlimited.uk@gmail.com**.
